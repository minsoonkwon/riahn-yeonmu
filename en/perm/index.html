From parted & shadow perms
to volume straightening

At RIAHN Hair Suwon Yeonmu, a men's basic perm is ₩72,000, and a men's iron perm or men's volume straightening ₩120,000. For women, a basic perm is ₩80,000, a setting or digital perm ₩96,000, straightening ₩104,000, volume straightening ₩120,000 and straightening + setting perm ₩150,000.

Director Jihyeon's men's basic perm (₩85,000) includes a cut and blow-dry. We offer one-on-one consultations that consider your face shape, hair texture and lifestyle. Bleached hair may be difficult to perm, so please note how many times you have bleached in the last two years when booking. To change your color as well, see Hair Color.

Recommended for

  • Anyone wanting natural volume with a parted or shadow perm
  • Anyone wanting smooth hair with volume through volume straightening or straightening + setting perm
  • Anyone wanting strong waves such as a hippie or jelly perm
  • Anyone who wants to tame puffy sides with a down perm

Perm FAQ

How much is volume straightening?
Volume straightening is ₩120,000 for both men and women, and women's straightening + setting perm is ₩150,000.
How much is a men's perm?
A men's basic perm is ₩72,000 and a men's iron perm ₩120,000. Director Jihyeon's men's basic perm is ₩85,000 including a cut and blow-dry.
Is there a surcharge for long hair?
Yes. For women's perms and straightening, below the shoulders is +₩40,000 and below the chest +₩60,000. Please confirm the exact amount at your consultation.
