Layered cuts
shaped to your face

At RIAHN Hair Suwon Yeonmu, a men's cut is ₩20,000 and a women's cut ₩22,000 (shampoo ₩10,000 extra). Student cuts (ages 8–19) and kids' cuts (age 7 and under) are ₩20,000, and first-visit guests pay ₩15,000 for a men's cut and ₩18,000 for a women's cut.

The men's cut + side down perm is ₩40,000; with Director Jihyeon it is ₩45,000 and includes a partial down design perm on the front and top. We offer one-on-one consultations that consider your face shape, hair texture and lifestyle. For added volume, see Perms.

Recommended for

  • Anyone wanting a layered cut that suits their face shape
  • Men who want a cut and a side down perm in one visit
  • Students and parents looking for a student or kids' cut
  • Anyone who wants iron styling before an important occasion

Haircut FAQ

How much is a men's cut?
A men's cut is ₩20,000, or ₩15,000 for first-visit guests.
How much is a women's cut?
A women's cut is ₩22,000, or ₩18,000 for first-visit guests. Shampoo is ₩10,000 extra.
Can I get a men's cut and down perm together?
Yes. The men's cut + side down perm is ₩40,000. With Director Jihyeon it is ₩45,000 including a partial down design perm on the front and top, and a back down perm is ₩15,000 extra.
