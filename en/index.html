About

In Yeonmu-dong, Suwon,
RIAHN Hair Suwon Yeonmu

RIAHN Hair Suwon Yeonmu offers a wide range of services, including layered cuts, parted perms, shadow perms and volume straightening. We offer one-on-one consultations that consider your face shape, hair texture and lifestyle.

Director Jihyeon and senior designers Hanseol and Yujeong each suggest styles from their own specialties. From cuts to perms and color, it is a salon where you can consult in a relaxed atmosphere.

Opened in 2024 with 12 styling chairs, the salon is at 2F, 189 Changnyong-daero, Jangan-gu, Suwon-si. Free parking is available, but the underground lot is small and parking can be difficult. If you are coming by car, please call before your visit. We can direct you to the parking lot of Daiso Suwon Yeonmu nearby as an alternative.

Why RIAHN Hair Suwon Yeonmu

  • Tailored consultations that consider your face shape and hair texture, for a natural change.
  • Designers with a range of specialties, including layered cuts, parted perms and shadow perms.
  • Many styling photos and reviews left by our visitors.
  • On your first visit only, services are 30% off (excluding cuts, blow-dry and partial services).
