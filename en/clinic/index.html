Treatments & Scalp Care

Prices for our basic treatment (PPT / LPP) and premium scalp care. Prices vary with hair length.

Home/Treatments & Scalp Care

From dry, frizzy hair
to scalp care

At RIAHN Hair Suwon Yeonmu, the basic treatment (PPT / LPP) uses protein and moisturizing ingredients to smooth dry, frizzy hair, at ₩20,000–₩40,000 depending on length. Premium scalp care looks after flakes and moisture on the scalp, at ₩64,000–₩96,000 depending on length.

If you are getting color, there is also a women's cut + full color + treatment package (₩92,000). For scalp care with Manager Iju, please book by phone at the salon (+82-31-247-1121) rather than Naver Booking. See Hair Color for the color menu.

Recommended for

  • Anyone concerned about dry, frizzy hair after a perm or color
  • Anyone concerned about scalp flakes and dryness
  • Anyone who wants color and a treatment in one visit

Treatment FAQ

How much is the basic treatment?
The basic treatment (PPT / LPP) is S ₩20,000, M ₩30,000 and L ₩40,000 by hair length.
How much is scalp care?
Premium scalp care is S ₩64,000, M ₩80,000 and L ₩96,000 by hair length.
Can I get color and a treatment together?
Yes. The women's cut + full color + treatment package is ₩92,000. The long hair surcharge is extra.
